# Checklists – "Node by Node"

## 1. First installation (chapter 2, Appendix B)
- [ ] I know my hardware: how much VRAM and RAM (comfortable: 8 GB VRAM, 32 GB RAM).
- [ ] I have at least 30 GB of free space on an SSD.
- [ ] ComfyUI opens in the browser without critical errors.
- [ ] The Manager is visible in the top bar (Portable: `--enable-manager`).
- [ ] `sd_xl_base_1.0.safetensors` is in `models/checkpoints` and shows up in Load Checkpoint.
- [ ] W01 saves an image in `output/node_by_node/W01`; I've written down the time.
- [ ] I've taken my first snapshot in the Manager.

## 2. A good workflow (chapter 14, Appendix C)
- [ ] The workflow's goal can be described in one sentence.
- [ ] I know where the inputs and the most important settings are.
- [ ] Nodes are grouped, with clear titles and a note.
- [ ] Models are in their folders, licenses are in the register.
- [ ] Required extensions are listed (ideally: none).
- [ ] The JSON is saved; there's a working version and a STABLE version.

## 3. Quality control before sending (chapter 17)
- [ ] The image delivers the goal: what should it do for the viewer?
- [ ] Thumbnail test (200 px), phone test, stranger test.
- [ ] Checked at 100%: hands, faces, text, logos, product edges, background, shadows.
- [ ] Text added outside the model (don't rely on AI lettering).
- [ ] Format fits the platform; important elements are away from the edges.
- [ ] Resolution (print: 300 px per inch), compression, file name.

## 4. Law and licenses before publishing (chapter 18)
- [ ] Every model, LoRA and ControlNet: license checked and it allows this project.
- [ ] No real people without consent, no children, no well-known characters or brands.
- [ ] No living artists' names in the prompt.
- [ ] Realistic content that could pass as real → labeled as AI.
- [ ] The platform's AI rules checked on the day of publishing.
- [ ] Agreements with the client in writing (use of AI, rights to references, scope).
